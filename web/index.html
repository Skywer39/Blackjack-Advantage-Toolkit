Ramp & Ruin

bankroll and bet-spread analysis for card counting
Rules & bankroll

Every figure is computed in your browser from the rules above — nothing is looked up, and nothing is sent anywhere. The edge model is linear in the true count and slightly conservative at high counts; heat, backoffs and betting errors are not modelled, so treat every win rate as an upper bound. Card counting is legal; casinos are private property and may refuse service.
